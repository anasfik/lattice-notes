## Lucide icons

The icons of the interface are a subset of
[Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (package `lucide-static`, version 1.60.0),
published under the ISC licence. The path data of every icon is copied from
the upstream files of that package without a change and is bundled with the
product: the app never asks the network for a picture.

The full text of the ISC licence of Lucide, together with the MIT licence of
the Feather icons that some of the Lucide icons are derived from, is in
`src/components/icons/LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt`.
